## How 16 weeks became 13

The plan runs 92 days, so it follows the outline's 12-week compression (§18) plus a taper week modelled on Track A's final week.

| Weeks | UG (Track A, compressed) | PG (Track B, compressed) |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | Orientation, diagnostic, application prep | Orientation, diagnostic, discipline choice |
| 1–3 | Foundation: drawing L0–L2, diary, first builds | 1–2 Domain grounding; 3 Framing |
| 4–6 | Visual thinking and ideation, ten-in-ten, explanation cards | 4 Framing; 5–6 Domain exercises |
| 7–9 | Concept and context, weekly full simulation, making drills | 7–8 Field and evidence; 9 Simulation |
| 10–12 | Mains weighting, rehearsed conversations, weak-area repair | 10 Simulation; 11–12 Portfolio and interview |
| 13 | Taper | Taper |

## What changed from the first tracker prototype

- Removed all GK, reasoning, English and quantitative drills (outline §01).
- Replaced weekly mocks from day 2 with the operating system: timed briefs first, full simulations from week 7 (week 4 for the sharpening band), each with the same-day review protocol (outline §15).
- Replaced "Mock day, review day" weekends with the outline's week: drill six days, three build blocks, Thursday critique, Saturday simulation, Sunday rest and written review.
- Added the diagnostic, bands, weak-axis blocks, error ledger with four buckets, design diary, building drills, explanation cards, awareness index cards, rehearsed conversations and portfolio work.
- Renamed the UG track, replaced the invented M.Des stream clusters with NID's six discipline groups, and added the combination rules.
- Corrected every date and time to the handbooks.
